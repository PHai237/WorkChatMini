# AI Story — Literary Quality Trial (WorkChat 2.32.0)

The existing self-tests cover persistence, continuity and basic prompt contracts. They do **not** prove that prose is enjoyable or comparable to a human novelist.

For a useful manual trial, create one throwaway Story (not the manuscript you care about), set Manuscript to **Review before commit**, and write five consecutive chapters using **Continue Story**. Alternate a grounded character scene, a consequential scene, a quiet aftermath, an encounter and a choice. Leave Story Director optional; do not turn it on simply to produce more twists.

After each chapter, assess these on a 0–2 scale (0 = weak; 1 = mixed; 2 = convincing):

1. **Character agency:** Does the scene emerge from what someone wants, fears or chooses, rather than random surprises?
2. **Continuity:** Are knowledge, relationships, location and prior consequences consistent?
3. **Prose:** Does the language sound natural, concrete and non-repetitive in the desired tone?
4. **Dialogue:** Does each speaker have a distinct intention, even without distinct verbal tics?
5. **Scene payoff:** Does something meaningful change by the end, including quiet changes?
6. **Reader curiosity:** Would you continue reading for character/plot reasons, not merely a forced cliffhanger?

If a chapter scores poorly, use the existing **Chapter craft feedback** setting to identify the primary problem and compare the *next* chapter. Do not switch several preferences at once. Keep the generated draft separate from canon until you are satisfied. Use **Commit to Story** only when the manuscript is acceptable.

The new cross-chapter style watch detects only **exact repeated opening/ending wording**. It cannot judge weak metaphors, generic prose or subtle thematic repetition on its own. The final verdict must come from reading the actual chapters.
