# v2.28.4 — Auto Story / Continue Story

## Purpose
Make AI Story useful when the creator has little or no plot idea. Explicit Story Actions remain the precision tools; Continue Story becomes the low-effort creative path.

## Flow
`Continue Story -> Recall -> optional Research -> private Draft Preparation -> Draft -> Editor -> Final Continuity -> Bookkeeping`

The private plan is disposable. Only facts actually present in the final narrative are eligible for durable Story Memory.

## Autonomy
- Guided: stay close to creator direction and established threads.
- Balanced (default): fill in scene-level decisions and advance relevant threads without casual major canon changes.
- Autonomous: choose the next meaningful direction and invent fitting developments, while confirmed canon and continuity remain hard constraints.

## Memory behavior
Continue Story builds a retrieval query from the creator's short message plus recent chapter summaries, open threads, and character goals/state. This lets short instructions retrieve useful context without requiring the creator to restate names and plot facts.

Bookkeeping explicitly extracts the after-scene state from final prose and ignores private plans/research/discarded possibilities.
