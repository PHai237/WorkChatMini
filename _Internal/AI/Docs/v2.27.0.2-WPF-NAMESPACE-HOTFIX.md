# v2.27.0.2 — WPF namespace ambiguity compile hotfix

Fixes the four compiler errors reported after the v2.27.0.1 XAML hotfix:

- `StoryPanel.Ui.cs`: qualify the two event-source `Button` pattern matches as `System.Windows.Controls.Button`.
- `StoryPanel.xaml.cs`: qualify the Story avatar `Image` construction and cast as `System.Windows.Controls.Image`.

Why: WorkChat Mini intentionally enables both WPF and WinForms, and implicit usings make `Button` ambiguous; System.Drawing also makes `Image` ambiguous. This hotfix selects the WPF controls explicitly without changing behavior or layout.

No Story Memory, provider, bookkeeping, browser, Discord, notification, or performance logic is changed.
