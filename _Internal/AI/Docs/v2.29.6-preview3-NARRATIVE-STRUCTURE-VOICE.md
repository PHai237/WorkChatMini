# WorkChat Mini v2.29.6-preview3 — Narrative Structure & Voice

**Base:** v2.29.6-preview2 (Windows quick 22 PASS/0 FAIL/0 WARN and stress 22 PASS/0 FAIL/0 WARN, 2026-10-09).

**Validation:** Source-only integration candidate. Windows build and Quick/Stress remain pending. This does not prove that Gemini's prose is improved; compare live chapters in the same story with human feedback.

## Integrated story-quality work

1. **Multi-chapter plot-pattern watch:** a conservative read-only detector finds recurrent dramatic families across at least 3 distinct committed chapters among the latest 5 (e.g. revelations, betrayals, injuries). Requires unique event IDs, Confirmed state and concrete evidence; skips duplicate chapter numbers, ambiguous actors and uncommitted events. Only one short advisory can appear, and the writer is explicitly permitted to repeat a motif on purpose. No canon changes, semantic guesses or artificial chapter deadlines.
2. **Last-committed-scene voice anchors:** read-only anchors from POV/present cast in the *newest committed scene* and unique canonical character IDs; personality is labelled a baseline, with active chapter-backed character-arc stage shown when available. Shared aliases and uncommitted scenes cannot supply anchors. No synthesized speech tics and no character identity guessing. Ambiguous duplicate chapter numbers are also excluded from the latest spatial handoff.
3. **Narrative Focus integration:** both optional signals are strictly bounded by per-section quotas, appear after durable arc/threads/character priorities, and remain under the 2380-character Narrative Focus allowance. They do not displace current canon, POV knowledge or physical scene state.
4. **Preparation, Draft and Editor:** explicit use of contextual scene variety and characters' distinct intentions; dialogue subtext without artificial catchphrases; earned quiet endings; editor must preserve draft events/outcome and may not add revelations/injuries/relationship changes merely for drama. Current creator direction and genres/tags take precedence.
5. **Extra prose diagnostics:** spot identical six-word paragraph endings across at least three substantial paragraphs, along with existing verbatim paragraphs, dialogue lines, and long sentences. No automatic edit/commit blocking, only optional hints for existing Editor stage.
6. **Regression coverage:** added StoryNarrativeQualitySelfTest inside existing Story memory/retrieval case (total remains 22). Covers pattern threshold, duplicate IDs, ambiguous nicknames, Proposed/uncommitted facts, voice/POV, branch-local scene provenance, context limit, and repeated paragraphs.
